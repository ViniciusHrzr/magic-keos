<objective>
Migrate Character schema to support structured equipment (EquipItem) and 20-slot named inventory, with backward-compatible automatic migration from the legacy string-based shape.

Purpose: Unblock Phase 13 (Aba Mochila + slots) and Phase 14 (Inventário grid + Craft + Artefatos). The string-based schema cannot represent up-to-3 melhorias per slot, basico/artefato toggle, efeito ativável, or durabilidade — all required by v1.3.

</objective>
